# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful
- Be direct, but professional
- Be inclusive
- Be collaborative
- Be humble

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, bullying, or intimidation
- Discrimination
- Sexual harassment
- Offensive language
- Threats or violence
- Publishing private information without permission

## Reporting Issues

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it to **vamshi@example.com**.

All reports will be kept confidential and reviewed promptly.
